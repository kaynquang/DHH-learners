SeeSound

See your sound 看見你的聲音

SeeSound is a research prototype that gives deaf and hard-of-hearing learners visual feedback on English consonants.
SeeSound 是一個研究原型，為聽障與重聽的學習者提供英語子音的視覺回饋。

It is not yet open to the public. Teachers and researchers who have been invited can sign in at collect.seesound.org.
目前尚未公開。受邀的老師與研究人員可在上述網址登入。
